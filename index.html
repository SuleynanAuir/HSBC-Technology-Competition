THE PROJECT

A forecast is only the beginning.

The real challenge is turning a market signal into a decision that fits the investor.

This project connected an LSTM time-series model with a Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) trading policy. The forecasting stage extracted price trends; the decision stage combined those signals with quantitative indicators, fundamentals and user trading history to recommend a personalized strategy.

As Technical Director, I led the technical design of this end-to-end loop: feature representation, time-series forecasting, state and action design, risk-adjusted rewards, and policy learning across users with different trading profiles.

THE FORECASTING PROBLEM

Understand what may happen next.

The LSTM stage extracts sequential price structure. Forecast quality is assessed with MAE and RMSE against an ARIMA baseline, keeping prediction accuracy distinct from trading performance.

THE DECISION PROBLEM

Choose what to do about it.

The PPO stage optimizes a policy over state, action and reward. It incorporates user trading history and a risk-adjusted objective, rather than treating every investor as the same trading profile.

How the reinforcement-learning problem is framed +

State

Quantitative indicators, fundamental features, predictive signals and user trading history.

Action

A trading decision that contributes to the user’s recommended strategy.

Reward

A risk-adjusted trading objective used to learn the policy across heterogeneous users.
